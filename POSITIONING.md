**For** metrics-literate but burned-out professionals who track their body, are quietly
sick of being scored, and suspect something's still missing, **Fiboprana** is the
**mind-state layer on top of the wearables they already own** — inner practice
(meditation, breath, qigong) and the body's signals on one screen, over weeks, so they
can finally see whether their practice is doing anything: quiet proof, not a daily
grade. **Unlike** body-only rings or mind-only meditation apps, Fiboprana starts from
inner experience and is trustworthy by design — your data stays yours, billing is
honest, gentle by default.

## FOR
- The tracker-anxious ("it tells me I'm never enough") seeking relief, not optimization
- Practitioners who can't tell if their practice is doing anything
- Burned-out high-achievers, metrics-literate and skeptical of woo AND of grading
- Device-optional people — manual check-ins are a first-class path, never a fallback

## NOT for
- Anyone seeking diagnosis, treatment, or a medical device — we are general wellness
- Optimizers who want a better score, a leaderboard, or a streak — we don't grade
- Woo-first audiences wanting energy readings or manifestation — grounded only
- Therapy-bot seekers — we reflect data, we don't counsel

## The wedge vs. the moat
The longitudinal mind↔body correlation is the wedge — contested (WHOOP, Welltory, Oura,
big-tech coaches) and copyable; never claim uniqueness. The moat is the stack: trust &
transparency, community, privacy & data ownership, practice-domain depth, the per-user
fused practice-tag dataset. Every touchpoint deposits into that stack.
