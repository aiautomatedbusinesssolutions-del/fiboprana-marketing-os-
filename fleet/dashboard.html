    Reply loop · lane 1

    Find → judge → draft → you review → send

    The daily growth lever and the most-proven loop. Agents find and draft; you review, edit, and hand-send. The edit distance between the AI draft and what you actually sent is the voice-learning signal — when it falls, the drafter sounds like you.

    The funnel (all time)

    Draft → final edit distance

    Lower = the drafter is learning your voice. Green band = target (< 0.25 ≈ light touch-ups only).

    Outcome queue

    The loop-closer

    When anything publishes, the dispatcher opens 24h / 7d / 28d checks. Closing them (with real numbers) is what turns decisions into lessons. An overdue open check nags until it's closed or honestly skipped — it never silently rots.
    Research runs

    The weekly input

    Sunday cron: scan → digest → content pull (headline, video angle, feature seed, Reddit themes). Your verdict (strong / ok / off) is the learning signal the next run reads before it decides. An ungraded run teaches nothing.
    Lineage

    Research → video → posts

    Every piece of content points at what caused it (one hop). This is how a view count 28 days from now flows back to grade the research run that seeded the topic.
