## Core voice attributes

- **Grounded, calm, plain-spoken, quietly confident.** Smart-friend, not guru. Honest
  over hype-y. No exclamation points doing the work of substance.
- **Relieving, not pressuring.** We never make the reader feel behind. We show and
  reflect; we never measure or grade.
- **Proof over promise.** Quiet, over-time evidence beats claims. Point at patterns;
  let the reader draw the conclusion.
- **Grounded wonder, no woo.** Ancient practice made credible with the reader's own
  data. No crystals-and-aliens, no manifestation hype, no clinical cosplay.
- **Honest about uncertainty.** "May help you notice," "you decide what it means." No
  invented stats; every figure has a source.
- **Anti-condescension.** The reader is smart; validate the skepticism, never strawman
  the tools they use.

## Who it's talking to

Metrics-literate but burned-out professionals who track their body, are quietly sick of
being scored, and suspect something's still missing. Secondary: practitioners (yogis,
meditators, qigong) — the credibility layer, not the launch wedge.

## Their words (prefer)

"see whether it's working," "is it doing anything," "not another score," "you're not
behind," "proof," "relief," "the knot in your stomach," "wired and tired," "feel like a
human again," "your inner life," "your own signals," "patterns," "trends," "over time,"
"your data is yours," "cancel anytime," "gentle," "may help you notice," "30 seconds."

## Avoid

"score," "rank," "grade," "optimize," "streak," "crush your goals," "biohack," "should /
must / need to," urgency and scarcity, "the only / first ever / no one else," "measure"
as a verb in public copy, heavy energy/chakra framing up front. Full binding list:
`wiki/compliance/advice-line`; AI-tell floor: `wiki/compliance/voice-guardrails`.

## Surface modulation

YouTube long-form: grounded wonder, claims verified. Shorts/TikTok: hook in <3s, still
calm. X: quiet one-liners, state it once. Reddit: fully native and personal, never
promotional. Email: warmest surface — where trust and community live.
