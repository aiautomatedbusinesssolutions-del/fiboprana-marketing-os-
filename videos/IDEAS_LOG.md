## 2026-09-03 — parked from the quantum-mind video (founder call: one story, one question)
- **"They said it could not be done on purpose."** Evergreen mind-science idea: the 2014
  randomized trial (Kox et al., PNAS 111:7379, DOI 10.1073/pnas.1322174111) where 12
  people trained ten days in meditation, breathing and cold exposure showed a voluntary
  adrenaline surge, a faster anti-inflammatory response and fewer flu-like symptoms after
  an endotoxin injection, versus 12 untrained. The paper opens by saying these systems
  "cannot be voluntarily influenced." Pairs with the channel's tummo (2013) and
  long-term-meditator (2004) videos as the third "instruments arrive decades after the
  method" case. Verified via Europe PMC 2026-09-03. Say-with-care: no Wim Hof by name
  (abstract does not name him), no treatment language, no instructions to hyperventilate
  or cold-immerse.
