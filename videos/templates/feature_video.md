# Feature Video Template — "the pain, made visible"

> Fiboprana version (2026-08-26). The engine's feature-video template sold a shipped
> free tool; Fiboprana is pre-launch, so this lane's job is different: take ONE
> validated pain (from the reply queue / observations) and make the viewer feel seen,
> then show the shape of the answer — honestly marked as what exists today (the
> check-in idea, the lead magnet, the waitlist) vs. what's coming. Faceless pipeline:
> narration read verbatim by the voice clone, Quiet Earth visuals per beat.

## Hard rules
- **Never claim capabilities that don't ship yet** (`wiki/compliance/advice-line`): no
  live-integration claims, no "import your history" copy until import ships, no named
  device partners. "We're building" is honest and allowed; "it does" is not, until it does.
- Wellness-line clean, grounded-not-woo, device-scoped framing — same floor as the
  news template.
- **Target 3–5 minutes**, ≤16 beats, every beat gets motion, verbatim narration.

## Structure
1. **Hook** — the pain in the viewer's own words (a real verbatim from the corpus works
   best: "it tells me I'm never enough").
2. **You're not broken** — validate; name how common the pattern is (sourced if a
   number is used).
3. **Why the current tools can't fix it** — body-only rings, mind-only apps, scores
   that grade instead of show. Respectful: never strawman the tools.
4. **The missing layer** — the idea: inner practice and body signals on one screen,
   over weeks, no score. Show it in the Quiet Earth visual language (concept scenes,
   clearly stylized — never a fake product UI passed off as real).
5. **What you can do today** — one genuinely useful manual practice (the paper version
   of the 30-second check-in; noticing before checking the app). Value with zero
   product dependency.
6. **The Fiboprana thread close** (same block as news_video.md) + soft CTA to the
   waitlist/lead magnet + reply-loop question.

## Packaging
Thumbnail + description per `videos/templates/youtube_description.md` and the artifact
prompts (`content/video_artifact_prompts.py`). The pillar tag drives utm_campaign
(usually `quit-tracker` or `is-it-working` for this lane).
