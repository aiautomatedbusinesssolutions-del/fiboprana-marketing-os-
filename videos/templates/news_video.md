# News Video Template — "What moved in mind-body this week, translated calmly"

> Fiboprana version (2026-08-26), adapted from the engine's proven news template for the
> **faceless pipeline**: nothing is recorded on camera. The script IS the narration —
> the voice clone reads it verbatim — and every beat carries a visual direction for the
> stills→animation chain in the sibling repo (`Fiboprana Marketing/`, see
> `wiki/channels/video-kit`).

## Per-video production order
**research → verify → script (narration + beats) → stills → animate → assemble
(Remotion) → package (thumbnail + description + shorts) → founder review → ship.**
Facts are verified BEFORE scripting: never script off a digest blurb alone; every
scientific or news claim carries a named source the founder could cite. The
description follows `videos/templates/youtube_description.md`.

## Rules (non-negotiable)
- **Wellness-line clean** (`wiki/compliance/advice-line`): no cure/treat/diagnose, no
  health-outcome claims, no "measures your stress," no scores/grades/optimize framing,
  no uniqueness claims, no FOMO/urgency. Honest about uncertainty — "the researchers
  think," "early evidence," "you decide what it means."
- **Grounded, not woo; not clinical cosplay.** If the story involves a guru, a miracle
  product, or a panic headline, say explicitly what we're NOT claiming.
- **Device-scoped framing:** "mainstream wearables track the body, not the mind" —
  never "nothing tracks the mind."
- **Target 3–5 minutes** (450–750 words of narration, ≤16 beats). Every beat gets
  motion; no hold over ~12s; no clip reused (binding pacing rules,
  `content/style-kit.md` in the sibling repo).

## The first 60 seconds (retention scaffold, adapted for short masters)
- **BEAT 0 — cold open (0:00–0:10).** The verbatim hook is the FIRST narrated words.
  Banned: greetings, channel intro, "today we're looking at...", branding stingers.
- **BEAT 1 — make the stakes concrete (0:10–0:30).** Who feels what. One concrete
  number or named consequence, sourced — not vibes.
- **BEAT 2 — the artifact (by 0:60).** Show the REAL thing early: the actual feature
  screenshot recreation, the study's key figure redrawn in the Quiet Earth style, the
  announcement. If the story has no concrete artifact, it isn't ready to script.
- **Banned anywhere in 0–60s:** like/subscribe housekeeping, credentials, roadmap
  paragraphs, throat-clearing.

## Structure (7 beats)
1. **Hook** — curiosity gap + stakes, calm. Written VERBATIM (it's read as written —
   everything is, in this pipeline, but the hook gets the most drafting care).
2. **What actually happened** — the plain facts, sourced.
3. **What it means in plain English** — translate the jargon (HRV, readiness,
   interoception) without the textbook voice.
4. **The catch** — the tension / the fine print / what the study can't say. The heart
   of the video; slow down here.
5. **The bigger shift** — zoom out: why it's a pattern (neurowellness, the
   over-optimization backlash), not a one-off.
6. **The turn inward** — what it means for *you*: not a new protocol, a new thing to
   notice in your own experience.
7. **Close + soft CTA** — the Fiboprana thread (below) + a question that seeds replies.

## The Fiboprana thread — bake into the close of EVERY video
- **Setup (from the topic):** the industry keeps building better ways to score your
  body — and now wants to score your mind too.
- **The question:** a number can tell you something changed. It can't tell you what it
  meant, and it was never supposed to grade you.
- **The Fiboprana line:** the point was never a better score — it's finally *seeing*
  your inner practice and your body's signals in one picture, over weeks, and deciding
  for yourself what it means. Quiet proof, not another verdict.
- **Then** the soft CTA (fiboprana.com — the waitlist/lead magnet) + the reply-loop
  question. Non-preachy: a reframe, not an anti-tech rant.

## Visual language (every beat)
Each beat carries a one-line "Visual:" direction in the Quiet Earth kit: flat matte
painterly, sand ground, gold = mind/energy, pine = body/nature, faceless silhouettes,
one gold + one pine element per scene. Never photorealistic humans (disclosure-exempt
lane), never chakra rainbows, never score-UI screens glorified.
