## Provisional patterns (from founding research, not yet from the observation corpus)

1. **Score-before-self.** People check the app's verdict before consulting their own
   felt sense ("checking my sleep score before I've decided how I feel"). Evidence:
   press coverage of Oura "hangxiety," WHOOP mismatch complaints, orthosomnia
   literature. → quit-tracker pillar; the no-score design principle.
2. **Practice-proof hunger.** Practitioners quit because progress is invisible ("can't
   tell if it's doing anything"); meditation apps lose 90%+ of users in 30 days.
   → is-it-working pillar; the core product promise.
3. **Data-meaning gap.** Years of wearable data, no idea what it says about the inner
   life ("my recovery says I'm fine but I feel terrible"). → mind-science pillar; the
   correlation wedge.
4. **Burnout behind a successful surface.** "Wired and tired," "quietly cracking" —
   high-achievers fleeing optimization culture, seeking relief and meaning.
5. **Wellness-app distrust.** Billing rage, data-selling fear, black-box AI coaches.
   → trust/privacy as content beats and product principles.
