The production engine: faceless long-form (3–5 min to start) built on the three pillars
([[product/pillars]]), with Shorts cut from each master (9:16, 3 per video). Voice-led —
founder's own-voice clone over Quiet Earth stylized visuals ([[brand/visual-identity]]);
stylized/animated keeps us in YouTube's synthetic-media disclosure-exempt lane, and we
disclose anyway per the pipeline checklist.

Rules: land the hook fast; every beat gets motion (no static hold > ~12s, no clip used
twice); every scientific claim verified at scripting time with the actual study; titles
and thumbnails calm and curious, never red-arrow alarm; description carries the tracked
link (UTM per pillar) + disclaimer + synthetic-media disclosure. Discovery is YouTube's
job; the email list is where the relationship lives — CTA points to the funnel, softly.

Caution: YouTube's AI-slop enforcement can sweep faceless creators — originality
(original scripts, curated visuals, real editorial judgment) is the safety margin. Don't
bet the business on this channel; it feeds the owned list.

## The story rule (binding, founder call 2026-09-03)

Every video is ONE story with ONE question, told in two halves of that same question:
the **ancient understanding** (what a tradition described or taught, quoted from a
primary text in a named translation, stated as the tradition's claim) and the **modern
understanding** (what the lab found, verified against the actual paper, stated with its
caveats). The week's development is the doorway into that pairing, never the point: a
video is not a news item and never a roundup of items.

- Every study, case, or example in the video must answer the SAME question as the
  story. Evidence for a neighbouring claim, however good, is a different video: park it
  in the ideas log. Never re-use a case an earlier video already covered unless it is the
  subject.
- Never name or cover a competitor as the subject; category-level framing only ("a
  brain wearable now exists"). Competitors are tracked in the competitor map, not on the
  channel.
- Name the method, never bare "practice": meditation (default), breathwork, qigong, or
  the named ancient method.
- Grounded register: the mind is "stranger and more capable than most of us were
  taught"; capacities are things you can notice and train, never powers you are
  promised. No manifestation, energy, or "create your reality" language; no "science
  proves the tradition right" and no "the ancients predicted the science". The honest
  bridge is that both refuse the same reduction, and the tradition built a method of
  observation where the lab built a theory.
- The close is the turn inward and Fiboprana as the bridge: "may help you notice", "you
  decide what it means".
