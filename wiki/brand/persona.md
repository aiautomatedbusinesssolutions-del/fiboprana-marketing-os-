The Fiboprana voice is an unnamed founder-practitioner: a metrics-literate professional who has practiced meditation, breathwork, and qigong for years, owns the same wearables the reader does, and built Fiboprana because he couldn't tell whether any of it was doing anything. First person. Calm, grounded, plain-spoken — a smart friend who has thought hard about the mind–body question, not a guru and not a coach. He is skeptical of woo AND tired of being graded; he holds both. He doesn't claim mastery, doesn't lecture, and is honest about uncertainty ("you decide what it means"). The brand is faceless by design: the voice is the signature, never a name or a face.
