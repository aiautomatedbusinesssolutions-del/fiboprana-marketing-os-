**Palette:** sand `#faf7f1` (ground), sand-2 `#f4eee4`, ink `#211c17` (line/text/night),
**gold `#b07d2e` = mind/energy** (prana, Fibonacci gold), **pine `#2f5d52` = body/nature**.
The visual thesis: *mind = gold light/threads, body = pine/nature forms — every
composition is those two meeting on sand.*

**Primary style — "Quiet Earth":** flat, matte, softly-painterly illustration; muted earth
palette on sand; subtle paper grain; generous negative space; one gold element and one pine
element per scene; human figures faceless/silhouetted; slow, breathing motion.

**Type:** Fraunces for hook cards and pull-quotes; Geist Sans kinetic captions with the
keyword highlighted in gold. **Sound:** founder's own-voice clone, calm and unhurried;
2–3 reused ambient tracks at −18 to −22 dB under voice.

**Never:** photorealistic humans/places/events (keeps the disclosure-exempt lane), neon or
glossy 3D, lens flare, chakra rainbows or cosmic-alien clichés, text baked into generated
images, stock-footage compilations, red-arrow thumbnails, scores/grades imagery used
unironically.
