**For** metrics-literate but burned-out professionals who track their body, are quietly
sick of being scored, and suspect something's still missing, **Fiboprana** is the
**mind-state layer on top of the wearables they already own** — it brings their inner
practice (meditation, breath, qigong) and their body's signals onto one screen, over
weeks, **so they can finally see whether their practice is doing anything** — as quiet
proof, not a daily grade. **Unlike** body-only rings or mind-only meditation apps,
Fiboprana starts from your inner experience and is trustworthy by design: your data
stays yours, billing is honest, gentle by default.

**Category:** not another meditation app, not another health tracker, pointedly not
another score. The mind-state layer for the wearable you already own — software-first,
bring-your-own-device, works with manual check-ins alone.

**The wedge vs. the moat (internal honesty):** the longitudinal mind↔body correlation is
the wedge — real demand, good timing, but contested (WHOOP Journal + JMIR study, Welltory,
Oura Cumulative Stress, big-tech AI coaches) and copyable. Never claim it's unique. The
moat is a stack: (1) trust & transparency, (2) community, (3) privacy & data ownership,
(4) practice-domain depth (qigong/prana/insight credibility), (5) the per-user fused
practice-tag dataset. Marketing's job: acquire with the wedge, then make every touchpoint
deposit into trust, privacy, community, and depth.

**Messaging pillars:** 1. Proof, not another score. 2. See if it's actually working.
3. Built for your inner life, not your stats. 4. Trustworthy by design — your data is
yours. 5. You're not alone in this. 6. Grounded, not woo — and not clinical cosplay.

**Live hero:** eyebrow "The missing layer of self-tracking" + headline "See how your mind
and body move together."

**Never drift into:** a therapy bot, a diagnostic tool, or hardware.
