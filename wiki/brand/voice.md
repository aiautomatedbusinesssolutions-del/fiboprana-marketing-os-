How Fiboprana sounds. Load this for *how* to write; [[brand/persona]] for *who* writes;
[[compliance/advice-line]] for what can't be said.

## Core voice attributes (universal across every surface)

- **Grounded, calm, plain-spoken, quietly confident.** Smart-friend, not guru. Honest over
  hype-y. No exclamation points doing the work of substance.
- **Relieving, not pressuring.** We never make the reader feel behind. We *show* and
  *reflect*; we never measure or grade. The reader gets to relax, not perform.
- **Proof over promise.** Quiet, over-time evidence beats claims. "Over weeks, not as
  another daily score." Point at patterns; let the reader draw the conclusion.
- **Grounded wonder, no woo.** Ancient practice (meditation, breath, qigong) made credible
  with the reader's own data. No crystals-and-aliens, no manifestation hype — and no
  clinical cosplay either.
- **Honest about uncertainty.** "May help you notice," "you decide what it means." Most
  viral mind-content is misinformation; being the credible one is the edge.
- **Anti-condescension.** The reader is smart; many are more metrics-literate than most
  apps assume. Validate the skepticism, never strawman the tools they use.

## Who it's talking to

Primary: metrics-literate but burned-out professionals who track their body, are quietly
sick of being scored, and suspect something's still missing ([[market/icp]]). Secondary:
practitioners (yogis, meditators, qigong) — the credibility layer, not the launch wedge.

## Their words (prefer these)

"see whether it's working," "is it doing anything," "not another score," "you're not
behind," "proof," "relief," "you can relax now," "the knot in your stomach," "running on
fumes," "wired and tired," "feel like a human again," "presence," "reset," "decompress,"
"your inner life," "your own signals," "patterns," "trends," "over time," "your data is
yours," "cancel anytime," "gentle," "may help you notice," "30 seconds."

## Avoid (brand side — the full binding list is [[compliance/advice-line]])

"score," "rank," "grade," "optimize," "performance," "streak," "crush your goals,"
"biohack," "you're falling behind," "should / must / need to," urgency and scarcity,
"the only / first ever / no one else," "measure" as a verb in public copy, heavy
energy/chakra framing up front (practitioner layer only, where it's literal to a practice).

## Surface modulation (same attributes, different volume)

- **YouTube long-form** — grounded wonder at full depth; museum-quality, every scientific
  claim verified at scripting time.
- **Shorts / TikTok** — land the hook in under 3 seconds, still calm; curiosity and relief,
  never alarm.
- **X** — quiet observations and one-liners; state it once, let it sit.
- **Reddit** — fully native and personal; the founder as an individual. Never promotional.
- **Email** — warmest surface; where trust and community live. Honest, direct, unhurried.
