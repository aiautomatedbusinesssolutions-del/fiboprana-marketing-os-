- **Research before building.** Data and analytics decide what to build and deepen — never
  guess what users want.
- **Build thin, then deepen.** Ship the basic core of each module; expand only what's
  validated.
- **Faceless marketing.** No founder face; a consistent brand voice (own-voice clone) over
  stylized visuals. The voice is the signature.
- **Grounded brand.** Credible and down-to-earth; no conspiracy or alien flavor, no
  medical-grade cosplay.
- **Owned channels first.** Short-form is discovery; the email list is the durable,
  algorithm-proof asset where trust and community compound.
- **Quality over cadence.** A thin piece is a liability, not an asset. Compliance is a
  content edge: grounded, evidence-aware, honest about uncertainty.
