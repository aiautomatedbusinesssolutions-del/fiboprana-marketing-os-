A mind-state layer on top of the wearables you already own: log your inner practice (meditation, breath, qigong) and how you actually feel in about 30 seconds a day, bring in your body's signals (HRV, sleep, recovery) from devices you already have or by hand, and see them as one picture trending over weeks — quiet proof of whether your practice is doing anything, with no score, no daily grade, and no verdict. Your data stays yours (exportable, never sold), billing is honest, and the core is free during beta.
