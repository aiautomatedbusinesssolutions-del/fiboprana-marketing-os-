**v1 core (locked, web-first PWA — Next.js + Supabase + Resend + Vercel):**
1. **30-second check-in** — manual mind + body log (1–5 scales), minimal core + optional
   depth; device-optional by design. Sell: "No wearable, no HRV ritual — just you, ~30
   seconds a day."
2. **Fast first mind↔body pattern** — surface a real correlation from the user's own data
   quickly (import history or quick logs). Sell: "See a connection between your mind and
   body on day one."
3. **No-score trends** — progress in plain language over weeks; no daily grade, no verdict.
   Sell: "Proof you're changing. No score to panic over."
4. **Is-it-working view** — does your practice move *your* signals over time. Sell:
   "Finally see if your practice is actually doing anything."

**Rules that bind marketing:** history import (Apple Health export, Oura CSV, generic CSV)
is strictly optional and never a gate — never make import sound required. Free during
beta; public copy already promises "the core is free" and "cancel in one click." Live
device integrations are roadmap — do not claim them or name partners until shipped.
Beta = invite waves from the email list. North star: week-4 check-in retention ≥ 30%.

**Roadmap (do not market yet):** bring-your-own-wearable sync (Oura / Health Connect /
Samsung first), practitioner mode (correlation + credibility framing only), community.
