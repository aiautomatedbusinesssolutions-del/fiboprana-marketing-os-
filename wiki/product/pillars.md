Three lanes; every piece belongs to one. Pillar slug = `utm_campaign`.

1. **`quit-tracker` — relief from the score.** Body-tracking without a mind layer creates
   anxiety, not insight; we're the relief-from-grading side of self-knowledge. Tone:
   validating, wry, freeing — never shame tracking, name the feeling. Watch-out: most
   users feel empowered by trackers; speak to the anxious minority without strawmanning
   the tools.
2. **`is-it-working` — proof, not a grade.** People quit meditation/breathwork because
   they can't see it working; progress you can notice, without a score. Tone: honest,
   curious, gently scientific; relief > achievement. Watch-out: no capability copy that
   doesn't ship yet (e.g. "import your last 3 months" stays banned until import ships).
3. **`mind-science` — ancient practice, modern proof.** Science keeps confirming what
   contemplative traditions understood about mind↔body — and your devices only see half
   of it. Tone: grounded wonder, museum-quality, honest about uncertainty. Watch-out:
   every scientific claim verified at scripting time; device-scoped framing only, never
   "nothing tracks the mind."
