No em-dashes (use commas, periods, parentheses); single hyphens in compound words like mind-body are fine, the long dash is the issue. No "it's not just X, it's Y." No "in fact / indeed / moreover." No three-part rhetorical lists (two or four read more naturally). No closing summaries ("the takeaway is," "that's why"). No invented stats or numbers — every figure needs a source the founder could name. If a piece could have come from any AI assistant, rewrite it.

Name the method, never "practice" (founder rule 2026-09-03): in public copy, "practice" on its own is too vague to market. Say the specific method the piece is about: meditation (the default for now), breathwork, qigong, and, as the business grows, the named ancient method by its own name. "Your practice" is fine only inside the product where the reader has already chosen theirs; in videos, posts, and email it is "your meditation" (or the named method).
