**Adjacent incumbents (the mechanic is contested — never claim uniqueness):**
- **WHOOP** — Journal feature + a 170k-member peer-reviewed JMIR study; the closest analog
  with published evidence. Hardware-locked, athletic-recovery framing.
- **Welltory** — already correlates contemplative practice with physiology over time; its
  correlation view is the loved part. Practice input shallow next to a real
  meditation/qigong/breath taxonomy.
- **Oura** — Cumulative Stress; also the source of "hangxiety" backlash. Gates its API
  behind active membership. Body-first, score-first.
- **Big-tech AI coaches** — Google (~$9.99/mo), Samsung; commoditizing generic AI insight.
  Distrusted, opaque, privacy-fraught — our trust story is the counter.
- **Calm / Headspace** — mind-only content libraries; content moat collapsed; ~1.8–2.0★
  Trustpilot driven by billing rage. Their churn is our proof point on trust.
- **Bearable / Daylio** — loved manual trackers (4.6–4.7★) proving device-optional works;
  Bearable is illness-coded — the wellness-framing gap is ours.
- **Mortis** — is-it-working feedback but wearable-locked.

**Our seam:** rich mind-state input (practice quality across meditation/breath/qigong,
inner experience not athletic recovery) + software-first BYO-wearable-or-none +
transparent billing and gentle anti-nocebo framing. **Positioning rule:** respect the
tools ("works with the ring you already own"), win on what they structurally won't do —
no-score framing, practice depth, honest data ownership.
