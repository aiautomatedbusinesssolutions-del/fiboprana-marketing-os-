**Maya, 36** — optimized-but-empty senior manager. Owns an Oura and an Apple Watch; has
Calm but doesn't open it. Metrics-literate and increasingly *resents* being scored: "My
ring tells me my HRV is low. Cool. It tells me I'm never enough — and never how to feel
like a human again." Burned out at a multi-year peak ("quiet cracking"). She is fleeing
"another score," skeptical of woo AND of being graded — she wants proof and relief, not
optimization. Timing: "Neurowellness" and "the over-optimization backlash" are named
top-2026 wellness trends; Maya is the wedge and the moment.

**Her objections (and our stance):** "I'm done being tracked" → then you're exactly who we
built this for: no scores, no streaks, quiet proof over weeks, you decide what it means.
"Sounds like woo" → we don't claim to measure your soul or your stress; your practice next
to your body's own signals, plain language, honest about uncertainty. "Who's behind this
faceless brand?" → trust is the product: data yours and exportable, never sold, cancel in
one click, real humans answer. "I already pay for Oura/Calm" → those track your body, or
your mind, or bolt generic AI on top; we start from your inner experience, work with the
device you already own, and the core is free. "Meditation never worked for me" → that's
the point: you couldn't *see* it working. "No time" → 5 minutes at your desk; relief now,
not a second job.

**Secondary (not the launch wedge):** practitioners — yogis, meditators, qigong. The
credibility layer and Phase-2 practitioner mode; correlation and credibility framing only,
never "your enlightenment, measured."
