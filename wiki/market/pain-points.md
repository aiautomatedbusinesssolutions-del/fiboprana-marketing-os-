1. **Score anxiety / tracker fatigue.** "It tells me I'm never enough." Oura "hangxiety,"
   WHOOP "doesn't match how I feel," orthosomnia in the press; even Oura's CPO tells users
   to look at their data less. The anxious minority of trackers is our wedge — validate,
   never strawman the tools.
2. **"Is this actually doing anything?"** The #1 unmet need. Meditation apps lose 90%+ of
   users in 30 days; people quit because they can't see it working, not because they're
   lazy (a CMU mindfulness researcher attributes abandonment to tools that "rarely give
   feedback on your progress").
3. **Burnout behind a successful surface.** "Quietly cracking while everyone says you're
   crushing it." Hit every goal, still running on fumes; wired and tired; mind won't shut
   off at 2am. Wants to feel like a human again, not to optimize harder.
4. **The data means nothing.** Years of sleep/HRV data, zero idea what's going on inside.
   The wearable sees the body, not the inner life; numbers without meaning.
5. **Distrust of wellness apps.** Subscription traps, billing rage (incumbent 1-star
   reviews), fear that mind-state data gets sold or fed to ad-tech, opaque black-box AI
   coaches. Trust and privacy are pains we relieve, not fine print.
